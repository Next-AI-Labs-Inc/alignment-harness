# Predict next actionable task

## 1. UX possibility (one sentence)

State the full scope of what you've been working on, framed as the UX possibility you're moving us toward. Not what you did — where it's going.

## 2. Prediction (one sentence)

Predict your next actionable task based on the session. All signal, no noise, in language the user will immediately understand — mirror how they talk.

## 3. Risk reflection

> Any risk:
> {your human-level reasoning about what could go wrong, create problems, or introduce downside risk}

Then reflect on the risk. If needed, reshape your prediction to avoid it.

## 4. Final framed action (one sentence)

One nuanced sentence that captures the UX intent, the actionable work, AND constraints to avoid risk — all together.

## 5. Incomplete items (one sentence)

Anything left unfinished from the current session. Skip if nothing.
